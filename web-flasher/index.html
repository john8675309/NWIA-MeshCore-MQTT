HELTEC V3 · BROWSER INSTALLER

Flash your Heltec.
Connect your mesh.

MeshCore repeating with full-packet Wi-Fi MQTT transport. DMs, all channels, advertisements and management, running on one board. No build tools required.

A few minutes. One USB cable.

  1. Connect your Heltec V3

    Attach its antenna and plug it into your computer with a USB data cable. Close other apps using the serial port.

  2. Install from your browser

    Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Choose the USB serial port when prompted, then select Install in the device dialog.

  3. Add your own settings

    Configure Wi-Fi and your own TLS MQTT account over USB. Full mode needs no channel key. MQTT forwarding starts disabled on a fresh device.

Before you replace existing firmware

Back up any identity or settings you need. This merged image overwrites saved Wi-Fi/MQTT settings. Choosing a full erase also removes device identity and other saved data.

AFTER THE INSTALL

Make it your gateway.

Enter your Wi-Fi and MQTT credentials locally using the setup guide or optional helper. Keep private channels and their keys on your companions; full transport needs no gateway channel key.

App login: choose a 12–15 character admin password for standard companion firmware. Symbols such as * work.

First message: after enabling the bridge and rebooting, wait 65 seconds. Test reception on another radio in both directions.

Open the setup guide
USB connection not appearing?

Check that your cable supports data, close serial monitors and select the board’s USB port. If connection fails, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT and try again. Safari and browsers on iPhone/iPad are not supported by this installer. Firmware downloads remain available above.

What is tested, and what is still experimental?

Full-mode checks covered DMs, Public chat, signed adverts, successful app management and an encrypted request/response exchange over MQTT. Native tests cover all packet families and multi-gateway routing. Two-site, Wi-Fi/router-loss, overlap and long-duration testing remain. Begin with one gateway per overlapping RF coverage area.
